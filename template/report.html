Analysis Methodology

This forensic audio analysis employs a deep learning architecture combining Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (BiLSTM) networks to detect manipulated audio content.

CNN Feature Extraction:

  • Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) are extracted from the audio signal
  • CNN layers capture local spectral patterns and texture anomalies
  • Multiple convolutional filters detect artifacts introduced by deepfake generation

BiLSTM Temporal Analysis:

  • Bidirectional LSTM processes temporal sequences in both forward and backward directions
  • Captures long-range dependencies and temporal inconsistencies in audio
  • Identifies unnatural transitions between phonemes and speech segments

Confidence Score:

  • The model outputs a probability indicating the likelihood of manipulation
  • Scores above 50% suggest potential deepfake content
  • Higher confidence indicates stronger evidence of manipulation
